# V7 TL-HOC Remediation Report

## 1. Executive summary

The V7 TL-HOC rho=0.3 seed=1 smoke failure was repaired at the mechanism level. The final smoke run has `generated_flows=626`, `installed_flows=626`, `completed_flows=626`, `uninstalled_flows=0`, `installed_incomplete_flows=0`, `final_active_flows=0`, and `final_waiting_flows=0`.

Low-load non-regression versus `static-ocs` also passes: TL-HOC matches static-ocs completion rate, received throughput, receiver throughput, and completed-only FCT in the final smoke run.

## 2. Root causes addressed

- Multi-hop OCS host-route installation did not forward through intermediate groups when ingress and egress optical spines differed. The route installer now adds explicit intra-group spine-to-leaf-to-spine forwarding for forward and reverse paths.
- Admission could mark an optical route installable without checking data-plane support. OCS decisions are now checked by `CanInstallOcsHostRoutes`; unsupported routes are released and reported with precise waiting reasons.
- Finite-cycle stage boundaries could pause arrivals behind active flows without explicit counters or bounded recovery. Deferred arrivals, blocked stage boundaries, final active flows, and final waiting flows are now counted, and blocked stage boundaries are forced forward after the configured stage gap.
- Waiting flows were only retried on topology updates, not immediately after optical capacity release. Completion callbacks now retry waiting flows after releasing capacity.
- TL-HOC could leave optical ports idle while demand/backlog existed, causing unnecessary waiting and low-load FCT regression. The finite-cycle controller now augments selected edges with direct demand/backlog coverage under optical port constraints, and fills the full mesh when ports are sufficient.
- Metrics hid denominator and accounting semantics. Summary output now includes install/completion rates, uninstalled and installed-incomplete counts, completed-only FCT, explicit receiver-throughput denominator fields, total received bps, and access-capacity-normalized receiver throughput.

## 8. Remaining limitations

- The smoke validation is intentionally mechanism-focused at rho=0.3 seed=1. It does not claim multi-seed statistical confidence.
- When optical ports are sufficient for a full mesh, TL-HOC now fills otherwise idle direct OCS edges for demand coverage. This is deliberate for liveness and low-load non-regression, but it means this low-load topology converges to static-ocs-like direct optical coverage.
